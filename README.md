# Visual Progression: Meals

A RimWorld 1.7 mod. Meals made before your colony reaches the industrial age use rustic or medieval meal art, and
meals made after use the normal art. Old meals keep their look until they're eaten, so the change happens slowly.
The art comes from Rustic Meal Retexture, Medieval Overhaul or both. This mod contains none.

- With Rustic Meal Retexture: simple, fine and lavish meals, packaged survival meals, and Vanilla Cooking Expanded's
  gourmet meals, bakes, desserts and grills get its art.
- With Medieval Overhaul: simple, fine and lavish meals and packaged survival meals get Medieval Overhaul's art.
- With both: those meals use Rustic Meal Retexture's art in the tribal age and Medieval Overhaul's in the medieval
  age, except packaged survival meals, which use Medieval Overhaul's in both. The Vanilla Cooking Expanded meals use
  Rustic Meal Retexture's until the industrial age.
- With neither, this mod does nothing, and the game's log says it loaded no content.

[Steam Workshop page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3834892583)

Something has to raise your colony's tech level as you play, such as Vanilla Factions Expanded - Tribals. Without one,
your colony stays at the age it starts in, so its meals never change.

Nutrient paste comes from a dispenser, soups and stews from a pot and ruined grills from a grill going cold, not from
a bill, so the ones your colony makes use the normal art. Pemmican, baby food and the survival meal pallet keep the
game's art in every age.

## Requirements

[Visual Progression Framework](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3834891951) 1.1 or later, and
[Rustic Meal Retexture](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3453364177),
[Medieval Overhaul](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3219596926) or both. Load it after them, and
Rustic Meal Retexture after Medieval Overhaul.
