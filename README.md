# Visual Progression: Medieval Overhaul Meals

A RimWorld 1.7 mod. Simple, fine and lavish meals and packaged survival meals made before your colony
reaches the industrial age use Medieval Overhaul's meal art. Meals made after use the normal art. Old meals
keep their look until they're eaten, so the change happens slowly. Meals that traders sell, and that other factions'
pawns carry, follow that faction's age instead. Nutrient paste always uses the normal art.

## How it works

It lists simple, fine and lavish meals, with their meat and vegetarian versions, and packaged survival meals
for Visual Progression Framework, which gives each meal its look when it's made. It's XML only and contains no art. It points at
Medieval Overhaul's own images.

It works with Medieval Overhaul's "Retexture vanilla meal textures" setting on or off. With Vanilla Textures
Expanded active, its meal art is the normal art, even with its own meal texture setting off.

Requires Visual Progression Framework and Medieval Overhaul.
