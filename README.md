# Visual Progression: Medieval Overhaul Meals

A RimWorld 1.7 mod. Simple, fine and lavish meals, packaged survival meals and nutrient paste made before your
colony reaches the industrial age use Medieval Overhaul's meal art. Meals made after use the normal art. Old meals
keep their look until they're eaten, so the change happens slowly. Meals that traders sell, and that other factions'
pawns carry, follow that faction's age instead. Nutrient paste from a dispenser always uses the normal art. Dispensers
need power, which usually comes with the industrial age.

[Steam Workshop page](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3834892583)

Something has to raise your colony's tech level as you play, such as Vanilla Factions Expanded - Tribals. Without one,
your colony stays at the age it starts in, so its meals never change.

## How it works

It lists simple, fine and lavish meals, with their meat and vegetarian versions, packaged survival meals and
nutrient paste for Visual Progression Framework, which gives each meal its look when it's made. It's XML only and
contains no art. It points at Medieval Overhaul's own images.

It works with Medieval Overhaul's "Retexture vanilla meal textures" setting on or off. With that setting on and
Vanilla Textures Expanded active, the normal art is Vanilla Textures Expanded's, even with its own meal texture
setting off.

Requires [Visual Progression Framework](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3834891951) and
[Medieval Overhaul](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3219596926).
